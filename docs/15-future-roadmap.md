# 15 — Future Roadmap

These items are explicitly deferred from the current hackathon MVP.

## Possible future capabilities

- Haptic feedback.
- Wearable enclosure.
- Multiple camera views.
- Robust localization.
- SLAM or 3D reconstruction.
- Improved spatial reasoning.
- Better object re-identification across sessions.
- Local neural TTS.
- More tasks beyond tea preparation.
- Multi-room environmental memory.
- Accessibility-focused UX testing.
- Cloud-optional acceleration with privacy controls.
- Production safety analysis.

## Future caution

Do not add these capabilities during the MVP unless the project owner explicitly changes scope. The hackathon success criterion is a reliable local end-to-end demonstration, not breadth.
